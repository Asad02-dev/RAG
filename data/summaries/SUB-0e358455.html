Executive Summary

This submission is for Acme Manufacturing, seeking Property insurance coverage for their primary manufacturing facility located in Ohio. The risk involves a large-scale industrial operation with significant fixed assets and specialized machinery.

Key Risk Factors

  • High Aging electrical systems and outdated wiring in the main production area, increasing fire risk.
  • Medium Proximity to a local waterway, which may increase exposure to potential flood events.

Underwriting Recommendation

Recommend proceeding with a conditional quote subject to a professional electrical inspection and a formal flood zone assessment. It is advised to include a requirement for updated fire suppression systems to be installed prior to binding coverage.
