Executive Summary

This submission is for TechFlow Solutions, seeking Cyber Liability coverage. The insured is a mid-sized software development firm specializing in cloud-based SaaS solutions, looking to expand their coverage limits following recent growth.

Key Risk Factors

  • High Reported data breach incident occurring within the last 12 months, requiring detailed remediation documentation.
  • Medium Inconsistent implementation of Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) across all remote employee endpoints.

Underwriting Recommendation

We recommend proceeding with a quote, contingent upon the insured providing proof of full MFA deployment and a completed post-incident remediation report. A higher deductible is advised to mitigate the impact of potential frequency in claims given the recent loss history.
