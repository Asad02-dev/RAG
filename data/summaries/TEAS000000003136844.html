Executive Summary

This submission is for a new Commercial Property facultative fire reinsurance placement for Beneficencia Española de la Laguna. The risk involves seven hospital locations situated across the Mexican Republic, with a total declared value and maximum limit of liability of MXN 1,813,122,000.00. The ceding company is Inbursa, and the broker is Marsh.

Key Risk Factors

  • High Significant aggregate exposure with a total limit of liability exceeding MXN 1.8 billion.
  • High Hospital occupancy presents elevated life safety risks and high potential for business interruption losses.
  • Medium Multiple locations (7) across Mexico require individual site-specific risk assessments.

Underwriting Recommendation

Confirm interest in the account and request the full terms and conditions slip and the detailed breakdown of values (desglose de valores) to facilitate a granular analysis. Given the high limit and the nature of the hospital occupancy, a thorough review of fire suppression systems, life safety measures, and specific location characteristics is mandatory before providing a formal quote.
