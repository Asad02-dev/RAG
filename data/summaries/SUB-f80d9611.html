Executive Summary

This submission pertains to Apex Logistics Inc., seeking Marine Cargo insurance coverage for their international shipping operations. The risk involves the transport of high-value electronics across multiple maritime routes, primarily originating from Southeast Asia.

Key Risk Factors

  • High Recent claims history indicates a trend of cargo theft during transshipment in high-risk ports.
  • Medium Inconsistent use of real-time GPS tracking on approximately 30% of the current transit fleet.

Underwriting Recommendation

We recommend proceeding with a quote, subject to a mandatory requirement for real-time GPS tracking on all high-value shipments. Additionally, we suggest implementing a higher deductible for theft-related claims and requiring enhanced security protocols at all designated transshipment points.
