Submission Overview

Insured Name: TechFlow Solutions Inc.

Broker/Sender: Marsh - David Miller

Line of Business: Cyber Liability

Effective Date: 01/01/2025

Intent/Action Required: New Submission

Email Summary & Context

David Miller from Marsh has submitted a new Cyber Liability application for TechFlow Solutions Inc. The submission package includes the completed application form and three years of historical loss runs. The broker is requesting a quote for a $5,000,000 aggregate limit to replace the insured's expiring coverage.

A critical point of discussion in the email is a ransomware incident that occurred in Q4 2023. The broker has proactively disclosed this, stating that the incident was fully remediated and that the company has since undergone a significant security overhaul. They are emphasizing that the current submission reflects a much more robust security posture than the previous year.

Key Extraction Details

  • Limits Requested: $5,000,000 Aggregate
  • Deductibles/Attachments: $25,000 per claim
  • Revenues/TIV: $12,500,000

Key Risk Factors

  • High Prior ransomware claim (2023) necessitates verification of remediation efficacy.
  • Review Needed Rapid revenue growth suggests an expanding digital footprint and potentially increased data sensitivity.

Underwriting Recommendation

Request More Information. While the broker has provided the initial documentation, the 2023 ransomware incident is a high-priority risk factor. Before proceeding to a formal quote, the Underwriter must request a detailed post-incident report and specific evidence of implemented controls, such as Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) deployment, Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) tools, and updated backup protocols. Additionally, confirm if the recent revenue growth has resulted in the collection of new classes of PII/PHI.
