Executive Summary

This submission is for Zenith Manufacturing Co., seeking General Liability coverage. The insured operates a mid-sized industrial facility specializing in precision metal fabrication and component assembly.

Key Risk Factors

  • High Recent history of workplace safety violations and reported incidents in the manufacturing sector.
  • Medium Proximity to flood-prone zones based on recent geospatial environmental data.

Underwriting Recommendation

Recommend proceeding with a formal quote subject to a successful third-party safety audit and a comprehensive review of the last three years of loss runs. Ensure that all flood mitigation protocols are clearly documented and verified within the final policy terms.
