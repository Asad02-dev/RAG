Executive Summary

This submission is for TechFlow Solutions regarding Professional Liability (E&O) coverage. The insured is a software-as-a-service (SaaS) provider specializing in cloud-based data management for financial institutions.

Key Risk Factors

  • High Recent history of professional indemnity claims related to software deployment errors.
  • Medium Rapid expansion of client base and headcount within the last fiscal year.

Underwriting Recommendation

Recommend proceeding with a quote, subject to a higher deductible to mitigate claim frequency. It is advised to request a detailed review of their internal Quality Assurance (QA) protocols and a summary of their most recent SOC2 Type II audit report before binding coverage.
