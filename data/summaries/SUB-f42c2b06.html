Executive Summary

This submission pertains to a commercial property insurance application for Acme Manufacturing Co., covering the Property Damage line of business. The request involves a primary manufacturing facility located in an industrial zone, seeking coverage for new equipment installations.

Key Risk Factors

  • High Aging electrical systems and outdated wiring in the main production wing.
  • Medium Proximity to a high-hazard chemical storage facility located adjacent to the property.

Underwriting Recommendation

Based on the initial risk profile, we recommend proceeding with a conditional quote. It is highly advised to request a recent electrical inspection report and a detailed site map showing the distance from the neighboring chemical facility. We suggest applying a higher deductible for fire-related perils to mitigate the identified exposures.
