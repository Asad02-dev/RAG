Executive Summary

This submission is for TechFlow Solutions, requesting Cyber Liability coverage. The insured is a mid-sized software development firm specializing in cloud-based SaaS solutions, seeking to expand their coverage to include third-party data breach protection.

Key Risk Factors

  • High Recent history of two significant data breaches within the last 24 months, indicating potential vulnerabilities in current protocols.
  • Medium Incomplete implementation of Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) across all remote access points and legacy systems.

Underwriting Recommendation

Recommend declining the submission at this time or offering coverage strictly conditional upon the full implementation of MFA across all endpoints and the completion of a certified third-party security audit. The recent loss history and current security gaps present a risk profile that exceeds our standard appetite without significant, documented mitigation.
