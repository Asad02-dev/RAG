Executive Summary

This submission is for TechFlow Solutions, seeking Cyber Liability coverage. The insured is a mid-sized software development firm specializing in cloud-based SaaS products and data management services.

Key Risk Factors

  • High Reported data breach incident occurring within the last 12 months involving sensitive client information.
  • Medium Inconsistent implementation of Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) across all remote employee endpoints.

Underwriting Recommendation

Recommend proceeding with a quote subject to a higher deductible and a mandatory requirement for full MFA implementation across the entire organization within 30 days of policy inception. A formal security audit report is also requested to verify current encryption standards.
