Executive Summary

This submission is a facultative reinsurance renewal request for Norte Energia S/A for the 2026-2027 period. The line of business is Power Generation, specifically focusing on hydroelectric power generation (UHE) located in Brazil. The request is being facilitated by the broker, Aon, on behalf of the ceding company, Fairfax Brasil Seguros Corporativos SA.

Key Risk Factors

  • High Hydrological and climate-related risks inherent to hydroelectric power generation.
  • Medium Geographic concentration within the Brazilian territory.
  • Medium Operational risks associated with the maintenance and management of large-scale energy infrastructure.

Underwriting Recommendation

Proceed with the formal evaluation of this renewal. Underwriters should prioritize a detailed review of the asset's historical hydrological stability, seasonal water availability, and the impact of climate volatility on generation capacity. It is also recommended to review the maintenance logs and operational protocols to ensure the mitigation of technical failure risks.
