Executive Summary

This submission is for Global Logistics Inc., seeking Marine Cargo insurance coverage. The insured operates a fleet of commercial vessels primarily transporting containerized goods across international waters.

Key Risk Factors

  • High Significant geographic exposure to high-frequency storm zones in the North Atlantic.
  • Medium Recent transition to a new third-party vessel management system, which may impact operational oversight.

Underwriting Recommendation

We recommend proceeding with a quote, subject to a detailed review of the new vessel management protocols and a verification of the current route optimization software. A formal inspection of the primary fleet's maintenance logs is advised prior to final binding.
